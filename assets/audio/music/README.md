# Music

Drop a royalty-free instrumental here (it is gitignored, nothing is redistributed).

Free sources with no account needed:
- Mixkit Free Music (https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/): free for social posts, marketing and YouTube. Read the license: https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree
- Pixabay Music (https://pixabay.com/music/): Pixabay Content License.

Then list it in your project's reel.json under "music": {"name": ["file.mp3", start_seconds]}.
Pick a start point inside a continuous, energetic stretch at least as long as the reel.
